    How this works
    • Vectors: . Every word is a 100-number vector; words used in similar contexts get similar vectors. GloVe is a close cousin of Word2Vec and gives the same kind of word neighborhoods.
    • Closest words: cosine similarity between your word's vector and all 30,000 others, computed in your browser. + and - add and subtract vectors, so king - man + woman lands near "queen".
    • Spanish: a Spanish search is translated to English first (miedo → fear), then searched. Translations: .
    • Map: PCA over the word and its 50–200 nearest neighbors, in 2D or 3D. Distances on the map are approximate; the list on the left is exact.
    • Coins: 10 for each word you search for the first time, 15 for rare words (outside the 15,000 most common). They're saved under your username: online for everyone on the published site, or in this browser when the page can't reach the coin server.
