    How this model was built
    • Training text: . Code blocks were removed so the vocabulary is about meaning, not syntax. Common filler words (the, and, de, que…) were dropped.
    • Model: skip-gram Word2Vec with negative sampling, . Words used fewer than 3 times are left out.
    • Closest words: cosine similarity between the full vectors, computed in your browser. The maps are a picture of those vectors, so distances on the map are approximate.
    • Colors on the map are 10 topic clusters found with k-means on the vectors, each named by its three most used words.
    • t-SNE keeps neighborhoods together; PCA shows the broad directions across the whole vocabulary.
    • Translations are a built-in English–Spanish dictionary for all words, so the page works offline. The model learned from both languages: English words show their Spanish translation, and Spanish words from the site copy show their English meaning.
    • Coins: 10 for every word you search for the first time, plus 5 for rare words. Clicking a word in a list or on the map counts as a search. Coins are saved in this browser only.
    • Read with care: this is a small corpus (about 7,500 words), so neighbors reflect how words are used in this project, not general English or Spanish.
